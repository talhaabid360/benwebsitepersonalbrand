# Ben Swann Personal Website

## Purpose

Create a standalone personal website for Ben Swann that explains who he is, traces his career from 1978 through 2025, presents his media work and directs visitors to his YouTube channel. This site is separate from the To My Sons and Daughters podcast website.

## Audience

- Viewers who know Ben from broadcast television or independent media
- Journalists, producers, event organizers and potential collaborators
- Existing followers who want a concise account of his career
- Visitors encountering his work for the first time

## Visitor conclusion

Ben Swann is an experienced journalist and media founder whose career spans local television, national reporting, independent media, awards, public controversy and continuing work through Truth in Media and related ventures.

## Primary action

Visit Ben Swann's YouTube channel at https://www.youtube.com/@TheBenSwann.

## Required content

- Supplied six-second desktop and mobile hero videos
- Supplied Ben Swann signature logo
- Supplied introductory tagline
- Complete supplied 1978-2025 timeline, including locations, descriptions and quotations
- Supplied press logos and marquee statement
- Supplied solo portraits and event photographs
- YouTube channel link

## Content constraints

- Preserve the supplied chronology and wording unless a correction is explicitly approved.
- Do not invent awards, employers, projects, statistics, quotations or endorsements.
- Treat controversial claims as biographical content attributed to the supplied timeline, not as independent editorial conclusions.
- The site must remain readable without animation.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop timeline uses a vertical alternating composition.
- Mobile timeline uses a horizontal touch-scrolling composition with scroll snapping.
- Hero uses the dedicated landscape video on desktop and portrait video on mobile.
- Navigation, typography, images and controls must work at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels.
- Reduced-motion visitors receive a static, complete version of all content.

## Release status

Build and local QA only. Do not publish without explicit user approval.
